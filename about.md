# About

<div class="about" markdown="1">
<img src="{{ '/assets/images/about_selfportrait.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Jean Fan with the antique brass microscope">
<div class="about__text" markdown="1">

This collection of Victorian microscopic specimen slides belonged to the late [Joseph Gall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_G._Gall) and was gifted by his wife Diane Dwyer to [Jean Fan](https://jean.fan/), who made this website to digitize the contents for posterity.

The antique brass microscope depicted here is the Weeham binocular microscope with separate eyepiece drives manufactured by [Husbands and Clarke Bristol in the 1800s](http://microscopist.net/HusbandsH.html), acquired in parts from Historical Technology Inc by Joe in 1978, and reassembled by Jean in 2025.

Tools and equipment used:

- Microscope: Nikon Labophot with a variety Nikon Plan objectives
- Macro photography of the slides: Nikon D3S with a Tamron 90mm f/2.8 macro lens
- Videography and non-macro photography: Sony Alpha 6100 with a Sigma f/1.4 lens
