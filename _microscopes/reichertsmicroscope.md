Carl Reichert (1851–1922) gained experience in microscope manufacture at the firm of Ernst Leitz and set up his own workshop in Vienna in 1876.

The microscope is pictured alongside its listing in The Antiquarian Scientist (Raymond Giordano) Catalog 30, Acton, MA, 1999.
